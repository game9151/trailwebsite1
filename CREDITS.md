# Image credits

## Illustrations (shipped, zero dependencies)

Every image slot on the page has a hand-drawn inline **SVG illustration** in a
blueprint/workshop style, built into `app.js`. These are original artwork
created for this project and carry **no licence restrictions** — use, edit and
reskin them freely. They display whenever a matching photo file is absent, so
the page always looks finished with no photo files at all.

| Slot | SVG shows | Photo filename (optional override) |
|------|-----------|-------------------------------------|
| Hero | Car raised on a two-post ramp, blueprint grid, gear + measurement ticks | `images/hero-workshop.jpg` |
| Service area | Garage bay with roller door, workbench and tools | `images/workshop.jpg` |
| Why choose us | Two stylised technicians (blueprint, no faces) | `images/team.jpg` |

Service tiles use original inline **SVG icons** (gauge, gear, brake disc, tyre,
snowflake, battery). Also original, also unrestricted.

  free to use; attribution appreciated and included above.
- If you add your own photos manually, record their source and licence here.
  **Do not** drop in images you don't have the right to use.
